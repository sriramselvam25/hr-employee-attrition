# Report Page Design — People & Talent

## 1 Workforce Overview
KPI ribbon: Headcount, Attrition Rate, Avg Tenure, Engagement, Avg Satisfaction, High-Risk Employees. Workforce composition and department distribution anchor the page.

## 2 Attrition Intelligence
Department attrition lollipop, role comparison, overtime split, tenure-band attrition and risk-factor panel. Selecting a department cross-filters every diagnostic visual.

## 3 Employee & Department Insights
Department × role matrix, salary distribution, engagement/satisfaction heatmap and employee-level drill-through table with privacy-safe synthetic IDs.

## 4 Talent Risk & Actions
Risk-band composition, high-risk department ranking, risk score distribution and retention-priority matrix. Action strip: Immediate, 30-day, 90-day interventions.
