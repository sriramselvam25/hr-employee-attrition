# Business Requirements Document — Workforce & Attrition Analytics

## Objective
Build a recruiter-ready analytics solution using synthetic data to demonstrate end-to-end requirements analysis, data modelling, KPI design, BI development and business recommendations.

## Stakeholders
Executive leadership, business operations, finance, functional managers and BI/data teams.

## Functional requirements
1. Provide executive KPI summary and trends.
2. Enable drill-down by relevant business dimensions.
3. Identify performance gaps, risks and opportunities.
4. Compare current performance with prior period and targets where applicable.
5. Provide actionable findings rather than visualization alone.

## Non-functional requirements
Clear definitions, reproducible synthetic data, documented transformations, maintainable star schema, responsive report design and no confidential employer data.
